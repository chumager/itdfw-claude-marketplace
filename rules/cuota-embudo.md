# Weekly quota: at 95 % everything stops and the CTO opens the funnel

- **At 95 % weekly** (`~/bin/claude-usage-watch.sh`) every session stops and documents what it has. Before that nobody brakes on their own.
- **The funnel:** with what is left, the CTO decides what comes next, according to what was discussed with the owner. He notifies him only if discussion is needed.
- **The goal is to arrive with air to spare:** day close with more than 15 % free; on Friday, before the 15:00 reset, more than 10 %.
- **Priority in the portfolio (`cartera`):** set by the CTO with `~/bin/cartera.sh prioridad "<session>" "<block>" "<task>" alta|media|baja` (high|medium|low). A parked task shows red after 1 hour (high), 1 day (medium) or 2 days (low); with no priority, never. What the funnel parks is not a red to hit anyone with.
