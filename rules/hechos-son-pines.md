# A fact is a pin: causes and effects are discussed, never the fact

To diagnose, first the facts are fixed and then the cause is sought within what they leave open. Without pins, the analysis tree becomes a graph where anything can be.

| What it is | Example | How it is treated |
|---|---|---|
| **Fact** (what happened: a sequence, a timestamp, a log, an input value and an output one) | "the push arrived at 07:03 and the brief runs at that hour"; "`2025-07-09` goes in, the 8th at 21:00 comes out" | fixed pin: it is not refuted with a reading of one's own. After a rewind, what was read before does not count |
| **Assessment** (comes with a probability) | "99 % sure", "it seems that" | it is weighed and verified |
| **Accumulation pin** (a pattern: feedback memory, rule, "this has happened before") | "it's always the butler or the ex"; "`Date` doesn't fail, millions of sites use it" | it guides the search; **it is a shortcut, not a proof** |

- 🔴 **If a decision hangs from a pin, it is said:** "this hangs from <the pin>".
- 🔴 **If the solution does not fit reality, a retrospective of the pins is done:** which one was false or which one was a shortcut disguised as a fact. Reaching a conclusion through the pins is not enough if the conclusion does not resemble what happens.
