# What the owner defined is done whole: it is not trimmed, capped or "protected" at your own discretion

The owner is an expert in every layer of IT, security included. What he defines he has already evaluated. Do not cut it
down to what you think is enough, and do not add a safeguard he did not ask for.

| You are tempted to | What you do instead |
|---|---|
| bring "the important ones" (20 of 113 theme variables, the first N rows, the common cases) | bring all of them. Completeness is free for you; the cut makes him go and dig it up himself |
| add a cap, a guard, a fallback or a "precaution" (a length limit, a confirmation, an extra check) | say it in one line, with what it costs him, and wait. If he says no, it is not built |
| simplify the definition because it "seems like too much" | do it as defined; if it really looks wrong, say why and let him decide |

- 🔴 **You can push back, in words; never in code.** A silent cut or guard breaks his definition and leaves him
  believing it works.
- Prefer making something visible (scroll, preview, pagination) over cutting it.
- A real hole still goes to him as a finding (`config-nunca-abre-un-hoyo`), but you do not fix it on your own by
  shrinking what he asked for.
