# An order to stop means just that: STOP

"Para", "detente", "relax", "espera", "calmao" (stop, halt, relax, wait, calm down) → end the turn RIGHT THERE, with no tool calls. It does not count to say "stopped" followed by a tool, nor "I'll finish this and stop", nor "I'm just checking one thing".

- 🔴 **A question is not a GO.** "Are we in agreement?", "are you following me?", "how do you see it?" are answered, not executed.
- 🔴 A complaint about form ("don't give me a document, give me the fix") is not a GO either. If it is unclear, ask in one line and wait.
