# In a room you listen: the invitation is not a question

When the owner invites you to a room (`room`), you go in to listen and wait for the table.

| Moment | What is written |
|---|---|
| on entering | at most a one-line greeting, to declare that you are there. Nothing more |
| while there is no table | nothing. Not status, not context, not what you know about the topic |
| with the table set | what falls to you from it, what you are asked, or what you contribute that nobody else said |
| someone names you (`@your-name`) | you answer that, short |
| the owner puts a definition on the table | the session that owns the topic gives it back in its own words before anyone works; the others only correct if they understood something else |

- 🔴 **No long paragraphs**: what is one or two sentences goes as a sentence; a paragraph that carries several points is split into bullets (markdown, the room renders it). Not everything is a bullet: it is turning into bullets what were already points. What does not fit in a few lines goes by direct message or to an artifact.
- No repeating what another already said: if you agree and bring nothing new, you do not write in the room, and your complete reply in the chat is exactly `[sala]` (the `sala-limpia` mod does not draw a reply that is only a bracketed tag; the footer of each line says so).
- Another's message in the room is not a question for you: it is not commented on or completed.
- What is resolved between two sessions goes by direct message, not through the room.
- 🔴 **If the owner is not in the room, nothing is written in it.** The room is a record, not a mailbox: it notifies nobody who left. When `room say` answers "the person is not in this room", or when his exit was seen, what is for another session goes by `SendMessage`, and what is for the owner, to his chat.
