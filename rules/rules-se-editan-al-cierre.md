# Rules, skills and memory are written WHEN THEY ARE BORN — the close queue is for order, not for quota

A rule, a skill gap or a memory that is decided during the day is written on the spot. A rule that exists only in `claude-docs/current/cola-cierre.md` does not exist for anyone: neither for the parallel sessions nor for me after compacting.

🔴 **A rule is a contract**: what to do, in tables or bullets, with its why in one line. The history (dates, quotes, the case that originated it) goes to memory, never to the rule: rules are loaded whole in every session.

Editing rules hot does not invalidate the prompt cache: the harness appends the diff and the live session reads it in the same turn. What does invalidate everything is changing the model.

## What goes to the queue anyway
What needs to be read by the owner in one go: findings for `bugs-cross-cutting`, memories that summarize the day, changes to MEMORY-TREE.
