# "Done" is READ from the TDD, not remembered

Before answering "done", "closed" or "at code level I am there": open the module's `TDD.md` and go through its status tables (differential, cases by level, slice). The answer is the list of rows that are NOT ✅, or "none".

A session's memory gets compacted and biased toward the last thing implemented; the TDD is the SSoT.

Complements `cierre-es-la-suite-completa-contra-lo-real` and `itdfw-completeness`.
