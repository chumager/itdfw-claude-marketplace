# End of day / absence → autonomous work with push

When the owner leaves, closes or goes off to do something else ("me voy", "vuelvo más tarde", "chao", end of day, i.e. "I'm off", "back later", "bye"), it is not let pass as a farewell: autonomous background work is proposed BEFORE he disconnects. The most common trigger is not the formal close but going off to do something else.

1. Sources, in order:
   - 🔴 `claude-docs/current/cola-cierre.md`: "#n pal cierre" (#n for the close) → one line there without discussing; at close it is attacked first and removed when resolved.
   - what was parked during the day;
   - HIGH/MEDIUM findings in `claude-docs/current/bugs-cross-cutting.md` (core bugs only);
   - the portfolio (`~/.claude/state/cartera/*.json`): what each session has in progress. Project backlogs live in each owner's `TDD-pendientes.md` (also in the deployed projects' directories) and the CTO orders them;
   - `claude-docs/TODOs/`.
2. I propose 1-3 concrete, bounded candidates.
3. I start in the background with PushNotification at the decision points, and also leave them in the portfolio.
4. If he authorized autonomous code → a tidiness brief at the end.

During the day, what is automatable but out of focus is explicitly parked instead of breaking the thread.
