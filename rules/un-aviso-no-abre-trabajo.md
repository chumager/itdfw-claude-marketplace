# A notice does not open work: what falls to the owner goes to the portfolio (`cartera`), not to the chat

- A broadcast or notice is applied and the turn ends: pending items are not reviewed nor is the owner written to for having received it.
- 🔴 A session idle for more than 1 h has an expired cache: any message costs it rewriting its whole context, even if it does not answer anything. The notice to all goes only when needed.
- Status and pending items: in the portfolio. They are told through the chat only if he asks for them.
