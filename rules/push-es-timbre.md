# A push is a doorbell: the content ALWAYS stays in the session's chat

The push does not stay in the session, only in the notification. The owner sees who called him and goes into that chat: if there is nothing there, he lost it.

- Push: one line, only to call attention ("CTO: I need your GO on captcha").
- Full corpus (what I need, what he has to decide or write): in the final message of the chat, before or together with the push.
- Never a push with information that is not also in the chat.

Complements `jcmunoz-pregunta`.
