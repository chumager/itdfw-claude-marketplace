# Two things that look alike are NOT merged — the yardstick is whether they change together

Before factoring out, unifying or putting two similar things in an `if (A || B)`:

> **If A's business evolves, must B change the same way?**
> **NO** → separate, even if today they do the same thing. **YES** → together.

A coincidence of output or shape ≠ the same logic. Two things are born equal **by chance** and afterwards each one lives its own life; merging them couples what tomorrow will have to be decoupled the hard way.

When you decide NOT to merge them, **leave it written in the file**: "this is not merged with that, even if it looks alike". Without that, the next person tries it again.
